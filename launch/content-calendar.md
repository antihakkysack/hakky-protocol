4. After another separate approval, update the avatar and read it back.

8. After separate approval, publish that exact proof post and read it back.
9. After another separate approval, pin that exact proof post and read it back.
10. After the one-way transition is finalized and canonical pool proof exists,
   obtain new approval before replacing the pin with the pool-live proof post.
11. Publish educational follow-ups only from the exact website, CLI, explorer,
   and canonical proof facts.

Each save, post, pin, or replacement requires action-time approval. Posts must
not claim a price target, expected volume, investment return, artificial
deadline, guaranteed safety, exchange support, or undisclosed promotion.
